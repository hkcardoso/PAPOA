About.

PAPOA connects design and visual development with the people, skills and specialist trades needed to turn an idea into a finished project.

The studio

One vision.
Many specialists.

PAPOA develops the concept, visualisation and project direction, then builds the right team around each job — from carpentry and upholstery to installation, systems and finishing.

This keeps the studio flexible while allowing each project to bring together the people best suited to the work.

Our team

Design, coordination
and skilled hands.

Our core team leads the project and works with a network of specialist trades. The exact team changes with the boat, the scope and the level of intervention required.
