Privacy Policy.

How PAPOA handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated · 25 September 2026

1. Information we collect

When you contact PAPOA through the website, we may receive your name, email address, telephone number, project location, project type and any information you choose to include in your message.

2. How we use it

We use this information to reply to enquiries, assess potential projects, prepare proposals, coordinate project discussions and maintain necessary business correspondence. We do not sell personal information.

3. Contact form provider

The enquiry form is delivered using FormSubmit. Information entered in the form may therefore be processed by that service in order to deliver the message to PAPOA. You can avoid using the form by contacting us directly at hello@papoa.pt.

4. External services and links

The website may contain links to external services such as Google Maps and WhatsApp. These services are operated independently and may process data according to their own privacy policies after you choose to open them.

5. Cookies and analytics

PAPOA does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies on this website. Essential browser or hosting functions may still process basic technical information needed to deliver the website securely.

6. Retention

Enquiry and project correspondence is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant discussion, project, legal obligation or legitimate business record.

7. Your rights

Where applicable under data protection law, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal data relating to you, subject to legal and contractual requirements.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@papoa.pt.
